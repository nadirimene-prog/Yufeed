# EU Supervisory Authorities for AML/Crypto Compliance

## Core EU Authorities You Must Monitor

### 1. AMLA - Anti-Money Laundering Authority ⭐ NEW
| | |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Authority for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism |
| **Regulation** | Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 |
| **Established** | June 2024 (published OJ 19 June 2024) |
| **Headquarters** | Frankfurt, Germany |
| **Operational** | 2025 (gradual ramp-up) |
| **Website** | https://www.amla.europa.eu |

**Responsibilities:**
- Direct supervision of high-risk obliged entities (starting with crypto-asset service providers)
- Coordination of national FIUs (Financial Intelligence Units)
- Development of AML/CFT standards and guidelines
- Monitoring cross-border risks
- Enforcement powers (fines up to 10% of annual turnover)

**Key CELEX:** `32024R1620`

---

### 2. ESMA - European Securities and Markets Authority
| | |
|---|---|
| **Website** | https://www.esma.europa.eu |
| **Established** | 2011 (Regulation (EU) No 1095/2010) |
| **MiCA Role** | Lead supervisor for crypto-assets under MiCA |

**Responsibilities:**
- **MiCA implementation** - Technical standards, guidelines for CASPs
- Crypto-asset classification and oversight
- Authorization of crypto-asset service providers
- Market abuse monitoring in crypto markets
- Cooperation with national competent authorities (NCAs)

**Key MiCA Technical Standards:**
- RTS on authorization of CASPs (Delegated Regulation 2025/XXX)
- ITS on complaints handling
- Guidelines on MiCA compliance

**Monitor:** ESMA's "Digital Finance and Innovation" section

---

### 3. EBA - European Banking Authority
| | |
|---|---|
| **Website** | https://www.eba.europa.eu |
| **MiCA Role** | Prudential supervision of significant CASPs |

**Responsibilities:**
- Prudential requirements for CASPs under MiCA Title V
- Authorization of significant CASPs (cross-border)
- Technical standards on capital requirements
- Cooperation with national banking supervisors

## Implementation Timeline

### 2024
- ✅ June: AMLA Regulation, AML Regulation, 6AMLD published
- ✅ December: MiCA partial application (stablecoins)

### 2025
- 📅 AMLA becomes operational (starting mid-2025)
- 📅 Full MiCA application (all crypto-assets)
- 📅 ESMA final technical standards

### 2026-2027
- 📅 AMLA direct supervision begins (high-risk entities)
- 📅 Transitional period ends for existing CASPs
